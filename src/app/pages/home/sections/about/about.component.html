03 — How I work

I like working where software meets ambiguity.

The best problems rarely arrive as tickets. They arrive as a CFO who can’t trust a report, a meeting where nobody remembers who promised what, a state agency with terabytes of satellite imagery and no way to ask it a question.

My job is to sit close to that problem, find the real requirement underneath, design a system that fits it — then build, deploy and keep tuning it with the people who use it. Frontend, backend, data or model: whichever layer the problem lives in.
